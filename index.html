Frequently Asked Questions

How do you protect proprietary prompts and model outputs from data leakage?

Every specialist operates under binding non-disclosure agreements inside isolated annotation environments. Client prompts, weights, and rubrics are never pasted into public AI tools or retained after batch delivery.

How are your Clinical, Dental, and Finance specialists verified?

We verify active statutory credentials before onboarding—including State Pharmacy Council registrations (B.Pharm), Dental Council registrations (B.D.S./M.I.D.A.), and CA Intermediate/audit qualifications—followed by a graded domain calibration test.

What happens if a batch falls below the 99% QA accuracy threshold?

Every row passes through dual-layer review before delivery. If any delivered rows fail your acceptance rubric, our pod re-annotates and audits the entire affected slice within 24 hours at zero additional cost.

Which dataset formats and schemas do you deliver?

We deliver clean, schema-validated JSONL, Parquet, or CSV files tailored directly to your Hugging Face, OpenAI fine-tuning, or custom RLHF reward-model ingestion pipelines.
